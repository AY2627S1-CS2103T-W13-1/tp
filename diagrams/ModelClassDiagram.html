Shared notation

Dashed line with a hollow triangle: interface realization. Solid line with a filled diamond at the owner: composition. Solid line with an open arrowhead: navigable association. Dashed line with an open arrowhead: dependency. Multiplicities apply at each association end. Only these four relationship types are used.
